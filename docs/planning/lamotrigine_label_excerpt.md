## Supporting excerpts

**Boxed Warning — Serious Skin Rashes (paraphrase):** Lamotrigine can cause severe skin reactions that may lead to hospitalisation and stopping treatment; the warning names Stevens-Johnson syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis.

**Medication Guide — serious skin reactions (paraphrase):** The patient guide lists peeling skin as a symptom that may be an early sign of a serious skin reaction.

**Medication Guide — timing (paraphrase):** A serious skin rash can occur at any time, but is more likely during the first 2 to 8 weeks of treatment.

The source label supports matching “skin peeling” to a serious-skin-reaction warning. It does not establish the diagnosis of Stevens-Johnson syndrome or prove that lamotrigine caused an individual event.
